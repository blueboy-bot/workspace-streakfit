# v0.38.0 posture and illustration review

Scope: all 138 movement records / 414 short cues, 34 previously illustrated movement IDs, both previously active teaching pairs. The audit does not mean 138 full-motion scientific demonstrations exist. See catalog-audit.json for exact coverage, REFERENCES.md for what research was actually read and the remaining source gaps.

Five independent AI contexts inspected actual rendered crops, without being given other reviewers' conclusions: pushing, pulling, lower body, core/all cues, and mapping/composition. They are AI reviewers, not five licensed specialists or five different model vendors. Reports retain their uncertainty and initial findings. Disagreement was resolved by evidence and limited use, not a vote.

## Findings and handling

- Bench and incline press: head/neck support unreadable or uncertain. Their artwork is withheld with a disclosed neutral placeholder; catalog exercises, planning, histories and recording remain.
- Regular pulldown: changing trunk inclination between panels was ambiguous. Two-phase lesson and contracted thumbnail withdrawn; first panel retained only with an explicit setup label. Independent pulling reviewer rechecked this limited use. Grip and common machine-facing cues remain, without claiming full-motion validation.
- Hip thrust, bird-dog, supported single-dumbbell calf raise and push-up: new static candidates; core setup positions explicitly labelled. No animation or exact-angle approval inferred.
- Hip thrust and deadlift showed neighboring-panel fragments, and calf raise clipped the head because generated sheets were not uniform grids. Explicit source rectangles now crop proportional square canvases with letterboxing rather than stretching. Independent mapping reviewer re-opened all six changed crop outputs and found the reported defects resolved.
- Seated leg curl remains withheld because the roller/support arrangement was unresolved. Other occluded hands, support points and dynamic phases are documented as limits rather than invented errors or certification.
- Cues clarify natural scapular motion, stable lunge step, handle/seat height, controlled end-range extension, neck/trunk control and single-dumbbell recording convention. The full instruction section notes that equipment, mobility and control affect range; no universal fixed joint angle is imposed.
- Existing English lesson mixed-script warm-up text was exposed during testing and translated as a complete phrase.

## Validation

60 automated checks passed: art routing/withdrawal, source crop bounds, bilingual catalog/cues, visual route, actual history editing, extra sets, persistence failures and foundation behavior. 16 real mobile browser flows passed (320/390 px, two languages, guided/experienced, green/blue). Two further image/offline browser flows checked exact assets, labelled placeholders/setup, withdrawn sequence, offline reload and unchanged logs. These software tests validate implementation, not biomechanics.

Existing versions and the production root stay unchanged. New preview uses the existing isolated preview storage namespace, preserving the prior preview data conventions.
