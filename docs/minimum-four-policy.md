Strength training targets now have a shared minimum of four sets. Automatic scheduling, daily readiness/time changes, resumption after recovery, replacements, target editing, current/future snapshots, backup restore and account hydration all use this floor. Targets above four are retained. Cardio duration and mobility/stretching prescriptions keep their own units.

The missed paths were the daily-adjustment accessory cap at two sets, the time-fitting loop down to one set, recovery resumption at two sets, and legacy/custom targets that bypassed the previous automatic four-set defaults. Adjustments now omit whole movements when a four-set block does not fit; logged movements are retained with an explicit over-budget indication when applicable. Pain, screening and fatigue stops remain in place.

Current and future low targets are upgraded and their prior day snapshots archived locally. Original pre-migration data is retained in a workspace backup. Historical targets, actual repetitions, weights, logged set counts, and historical share-card completion remain unchanged. Completed sets are never invented to satisfy a target.

Validation: the minimum-floor regression matrix covers 288 combinations of goal, experience, focus, time and readiness, plus persistence, manual overrides, replacements, recovery resumption, backup restore, historical records and completion. Related planner, adjustment, recording, card and compatibility tests pass. Mobile browser checks cover old-target migration on reload, normal-readiness preview/apply, rejecting a custom three-set target, retaining a six-set target, and a ten-minute adjustment that never creates a one-to-three-set strength movement.
