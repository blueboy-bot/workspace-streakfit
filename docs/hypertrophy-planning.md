# Hypertrophy planning defaults

The hypertrophy goal uses its own generator instead of the strength template.
Auto split: 2–3 weekly sessions use full body; 4–5 alternate upper/lower;
6 or more use push/pull/legs. Explicit split and available equipment still apply.

Experienced defaults use 3 compound sets of 6–10 reps and 2 accessory sets of
10–15 reps, leaving about 2 reps in reserve. Two-day full-body plans can use
4 compound sets. Beginners/returning users start with 2 sets and more reserve.
Control movements retain side-specific quality targets. These are starting
product defaults, not prescriptions or universal optimal ranges.

Plan at most 6 exercises (5 for beginners), fewer for shorter sessions. Count
warmup, 45 seconds of work per set (twice for side-specific movements), full
rest (150 seconds compound, 90 accessory, 60 control) and 90-second transitions.
Do not fill unused time with repeated variants. Missing knee-flexion equipment
does not create a second hip-hinge substitute.

Distribute a nominal 12 equivalent sets (8 for beginners) across the scheduled
exposures in the Monday–Sunday week. Primary muscle work counts fully; selected
compound secondary muscles count half for planning. Use actual logs from the
preceding six dates to reduce the remaining rolling seven-date allowance.
Unperformed future drafts do not count as completed work. Avoid another hard
exposure on consecutive dates; this date-level check is not an exact 48-hour
measurement. Individual recovery and actual stimulus cannot be inferred exactly.

Explicit long-term custom targets take precedence and retain budget warnings.
Never infer completed work or increase weights solely because time has passed.
Safety screening, pain stops and ambiguous historical weight handling remain.
Existing started sessions and historical logs are not rewritten. An explicit
Update recommended plan action can replace an unstarted plan; its previous
snapshot is archived first. The application remains visually minimal.
